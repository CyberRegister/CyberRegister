Licence not established

The files annotated with this identifier were already present in this
repository and carry no licence statement, no copyright notice and no record
of where they came from.

They are marked this way so that the uncertainty is visible rather than
hidden behind a project wide licence that may not apply to them. Nothing here
grants any permission.

Each file needs one of the following before it can be treated as covered by
the project licence:

  * evidence of its origin and licence, after which it should be annotated
    with that licence instead, or
  * replacement with a file whose licence is known, or
  * removal from the repository.
